# IFRS 9 Validation Checklist

## Data Validation
- Completeness of account IDs
- Duplicate account checks
- Missing balance checks
- DPD consistency checks
- Product mapping checks
- Macro scenario weight sum equals 100%

## Model Validation
- PD reasonability by product and stage
- LGD reasonability by secured/unsecured status
- EAD reasonability by product
- Stage migration review
- Scenario sensitivity testing
- Back-testing against actual defaults and recoveries

## Reporting Validation
- Exposure reconciliation to general ledger
- ECL reconciliation to finance reporting
- Stage 1, Stage 2 and Stage 3 movement analysis
- Management overlay documentation
- Approval and sign-off evidence
